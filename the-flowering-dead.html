About the concept

Where the idea came from

As a project made for the end of my third year at GameSup, I had to make a group project where we had to create a vertical slice of our concept.

Our core ideas were to create a feeling of gigantism for the Boss as well as make him feel visually disgusting and overpowered.

My process

From idea to playable level

STEP 01

Conception

We wanted a fast-paced gameplay that also included multiple characters, different equipment and abilities, and multiplayer accessibility.

So, while making the layouts, I had to think about how the map should adapt to multiple players with different strategies, depending on each player's choice of equipment and skills.

STEP 02

Layout

At the beginning I focused too much on how the players should move and fight comfortably in this area, but I started to see that it wasn't really looking like a believable city where people used to live.

With different changes and applying the feedback from our meetings, I obtained a version that is more believable while still offering hooks and debris to the player to move and dodge attacks rapidly.

1 / 3
STEP 03

Flow analysis

Since we wanted action to be the main protagonist, I wanted to instill in the player a sense of “state of flow” while moving around rapidly. The map can be navigated in a circular motion or transversally.

While entering the Boss arena, the player can trigger the fight when ready, giving time to plan a strategy with the team before engaging.

Also, the Boss is expected to have three phases that will offer a change in the action pacing. Depending on the movesets of each phase, this creates variety and requires adaptation while keeping up the pace of combat.

1 / 2
STEP 04

Blockout & iterations

I created an arena with three different zones in one of the first iterations. The zone around the Main Hall allowed players to gain height, cut lines of sight and hide to recover.

The Boss pit is the central area that allows players to attack more directly and move between zones and buildings. It's a double-edged sword, since it can be an advantage for the Boss too.

The third zone is narrower and has more buildings, but to avoid trapping the player there under incoming attacks, I used broken buildings to offer different levels of height to dodge attacks and counterattack.

The arena had to allow the Boss to also use its surroundings to its benefit, not be only a place hostile to its inhabitant.

STEP 05

Final level

Since the Boss is a deer with giant horns, I imagined how that would affect the buildings. While creating the destroyed buildings I kept that in mind, to show that the Boss is not just there for a fight, but lives and roams around the area.

Since it's an animal that has been infected by Datura flowers, I used a lot of vegetation to show the structural decadence of the city, and I needed to see how nature takes back its place without human influence. With the final touches, I had to reduce the size of the playable area and correct the positioning of certain buildings to obtain an arena that feels less empty and keeps up the flow.

The challenges

One of the greatest challenges of this project was to iterate a lot and fast. Since we had a big scope and expectations, we tried a lot and recreated a lot.

I learned that it's not a bad thing if something isn't perfect right away; it's usually necessary to see the next step to take.

Problems encountered

This was the biggest team project I had worked on. We had more time, but many deadlines and client demands.

Many members of the team had to rework what they made according to the client, and it caused delays on certain tasks as we reached the final stage of the project.

We had to adapt our planning and priorities to fit the deadlines.
