About the concept

Where the idea came from

We had to combine a game genre with an IP, both assigned to us.

Since we had Porco Rosso and Star Wars Pod Racer, we decided to make a racing game with seaplanes, aiming for the same gameplay feeling as Pod Racer.

My process

From idea to living universe

STEP 01

Scenario

The game takes place on an island off the west coast of Italy, only known by some pilots who have connections with the group that organises clandestine seaplane races.

The HQ of these events is the bar “L’Anatra Malandrina”, where all the pilots and co-pilots gather to challenge each other for money and popularity.

The player takes the role of these pilots, discovering their personal point of view on different themes: money, social status, politics, dreams.

Scenario / storyboard
STEP 02

Universe

I thought about how it would be if, at that time, people could escape the fascist regime rising in the country and live following their own rules.

The way I found to imagine that was to think about an island reachable only by seaplanes, vehicles that the army preferred not to use.

Paper map / top-down layout
STEP 03

Narrative Structure

The story is divided into chapters that follow the point of view of the different pilots. Each chapter is a story by itself, meaning the player doesn’t need to play the characters in a certain order to progress or understand the evolution of the story.

Each pilot’s story is linked by a common element that allows all of them to be connected narratively. Each pilot tells the story from a different point of view, giving the player the whole picture by the end of the game.

During the chapters, depending on the player’s choices, the co-pilots will have interactions with the player where they speak about themselves and their story.

Beat chart / flow diagram
STEP 04

Characters

I focused a lot on creating multiple characters that could offer different views of growing up in a Europe that’s changing socially and politically.

There are characters from different countries, with different backgrounds and temperaments.

Blockout screenshot
STEP 05

Narrative & Gameplay

I wanted the narration to serve the gameplay too: the elements seen in the game, as well as the gameplay mechanics, are justified by the lore.

Final screenshot

The challenges

Since it was the first time I had to respect a given IP, it required a lot of research to understand how to respect the universe and its elements while also creating something new.

Problems encountered

During the production we encountered delays and some management problems, and we needed to adapt fast. We also lost a team member and some of us were sick.

With a lot of work on the side and not enough people, it was hard to understand how to adapt and deliver a satisfying prototype without burning ourselves out to catch up on the leftover work.

We had to cut several mechanics we planned to show, to focus on the core aspects of the game and find ways to simplify what we wanted to do without losing quality.

We resized our ideas, chose the safest route and, meanwhile, polished what we already had as much as we could.
