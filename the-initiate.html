The game in a few lines

This is an action-adventure RPG in a fantasy world where a primordial energy that keeps balance in the world is threatened by corrupted shamans who want its power.

You play as a young warrior who fights against them with spiritual powers and close combat weapons.

About the concept

Where the idea came from

Avatar was one of the main references used while creating this universe.

I found the relationship between creatures, humans and an entity above them all really interesting.

To accompany the narrative, we also wanted engaging gameplay, so we decided to have melee combat inspired by hack ’n’ slash games like Devil May Cry, along with a skill tree for the RPG component.

My process

From idea to playable level

STEP 01

Scenario

To set up the scenario, I needed to create a strong foundation for the world itself. By creating a world bible, I structured the universe into different regions, where people live in different ways, with different climates, resources and creatures.

Now I had enough to work on multiple characters and develop a scenario for the whole game, exploring different personality constructs.

Scenario / storyboard
STEP 02

Universe

I liked the idea that all living beings are linked spiritually by a primordial energy. I wanted to create a type of creature charged with defending the balance of this energy between races, but this caused coherence problems.

So I reworked the foundations of my world’s origins and rewrote how the energy flows between races.

Paper map / top-down layout
STEP 03

World conception

I needed to create populations that survive thanks to cooperation with other tribes, since I wanted to reinforce the aspect of balance between races that help each other if needed.

I researched a lot to understand how humans function in tribes, how they manage hierarchy and rules inside their territory, and how they interact and exchange with others.

Besides, I divided the world into five different regions, each with its own characteristics, giving every civilisation distinctive features that make it unique and memorable.

Beat chart / flow diagram
STEP 04

Characters evolution

For this prototype I created four different characters: a main antagonist, his sidekick, a main character and a secondary character used mainly to give more depth to the main character’s backstory.

I wanted the players to experience a story of revenge and fighting injustice. To do that, I thought about how to shape the story and its events to capture the player’s empathy, giving a sense of belonging in this world.

Blockout screenshot
STEP 05

Cinematics

I had the occasion to integrate cinematics into the prototype to give more details about the story and characters’ lore.

I wrote dialogues in cooperation with a 2D artist who drew the cinematics, researched sound effects for the dialogues, and wrote a musical riff to sustain the ambience I wanted during the cinematic.

I had to make sure I communicated well with the artist, so that the colors and perspective matched my intentions and gave more credibility to the characters.

Cinematic screenshot

The challenges

It was the first time I created a narrative bible. It was challenging to think about the why, how and what for of the different aspects of the world: its logic, the characters’ behaviour, the tribes’ traditions, the geography, the flora and fauna.

Nothing was made just to fill the blank spaces, but to serve a purpose.

Problems encountered

Coherence was hard to maintain with all these elements that interact with each other. I wanted to stay true to the core ideas I had for this project, but I needed to discard some of them to preserve a logical world that works in all its aspects.

In some cases I added too many details that created confusion instead of adding flavour to the content.

Accordingly, I learned that details aren’t necessarily always good for the well-being of a project.
